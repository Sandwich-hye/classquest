The Solar System (Year 8 Science)
=================================

The Solar System consists of the Sun and the objects that orbit it,
including eight planets, their moons, and smaller bodies.

Inner (terrestrial) planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars.
Outer (gas/ice giant) planets: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.

Key idea — gravitational pull: the Sun's mass keeps the planets in orbit.
Planets closer to the Sun travel faster and have shorter years.
